This subject is very personal to me, as I myself have fallen into the trap of the workaholic culture that pervades the valley—so I can speak from personal experience when I say that it is _something to be avoided_, not only from the perspective of health + balance, but also from those aims which are supposed to be furthered by such a misguided approach.

#### in favor of balance

When you make the mistake of using too much force, it is easy to miss the forest for the trees. The reason in this is that when you never allow yourself to switch from execution mode → wandering mode, and so it is easy to lose sight of the larger perspective—said differently, a narrow mind is not prepared receive the sudden flashes of insight that can completely reframe the problem into the trivially simple.

#### negative work

I call negative work, therefore, the work that contradicts one's real aims—whether one knows it or not.
